# Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint

A two-part micro-confession addressed to Mom: a son who told her he quit his job for a break, when the truth is he was let go in March and has been pretending ever since. The addressee and the message need each other — the lie only makes sense told to a parent who believed the braver version. The concrete detail (let go in March) and the months of pretending carry the felt reason it stayed unsent: shame, and fear of worrying her.

`main.py` writes the exact two-part text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline.
